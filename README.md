# u-suroso-69-one-sentence-absurd-invention-pitch-5326c34a

One sentence, at most 220 characters, pitching a ridiculous invention that fixes a tiny everyday annoyance: a toaster that apologises for burning your bread and complains to the bakery for you.

Entry: `A toaster that apologises out loud every time it burns your bread, then writes a formal complaint to the bakery on your behalf.`

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes the line to `/output/entry.txt` (1-220 characters, one sentence).
